# 🚀 Introducing the Plans for Župnify

We're planning something new at Treved! 🇸🇮

Meet **Župnify**, a planned app that aims to take the idea behind our existing app, **Župnije Slovenije**, to the next level.

## ⛪ The Next Step for Župnije Slovenije

Župnije Slovenije was created to make parish information more accessible to Slovenian users. Now, we're planning its successor: **Župnify**.

Our intention is for Župnify to eventually **replace Župnije Slovenije**, offering a more advanced experience and additional features.

You can learn more about the current app here:  
🔗 https://treved.dev/zupnije/

## 🛠️ A More Advanced Experience

Župnify is planned to offer **more advanced features than Župnije Slovenije**, representing a significant step forward for the project.

We're currently gathering ideas, exploring possibilities, and planning the direction of the app. **Your suggestions and feedback are welcome!** If you have ideas for features or improvements you'd like to see, feel free to share them with us.

## 📅 What's the Timeline?

Župnify is still in the planning and development process. Our current estimate is that it could be ready in **approximately six months or a little longer**.

This is only an estimate, not a confirmed release date. The timeline may change as planning and development progress.
