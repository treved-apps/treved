# 🇸🇮 New app: Župnije Slovenije

We're excited to introduce **Župnije Slovenije**, a Treved app created specifically for **Slovenian parishes and Slovenian users**.

## ⛪ What is Župnije Slovenije?

**Župnije Slovenije** is a mobile app designed to make parish information easier to access and keep users connected with the life of their parish.

Instead of searching through old parish bulletins, websites, or different sources, important information can be available directly on your phone.

The app can provide:

- 📋 **Parish announcements**
- 🕊️ **Mass schedules**
- 📞 **Parish contact information**
- 📚 **Religious education information**, including the option to unsubscribe from catechism
- 🙏 **Prayer prompts and other content**
- 📅 Other information and activities related to parish life

## 🇸🇮 Made for Slovenia

**Župnije Slovenije is specifically made for Slovenia.**

The app is designed around the needs of **Slovenian parishes and their communities**, with the goal of bringing parish information into one convenient place.

Any parish in Slovenia can join the platform by requesting to be included.

## 🗣️ Slovenian only

The entire app is **available exclusively in Slovenian**.

There are no English, German, Italian, or other language versions. The app is intentionally focused on **Slovenian users and the Slovenian parish environment**.

## 🔒 Simple & practical

The goal of Župnije Slovenije is simple: make important parish information **easy to find, easy to access, and always close at hand**.

Whether you want to check the latest announcements, find the Mass schedule, contact your parish, or stay informed about parish activities, the app brings it together in one place.

## 🛠️ Developed by Treved

**Župnije Slovenije** is developed by **Treved**, a Slovenian one-person indie app studio focused on creating practical, simple, and useful mobile apps.

This project is specifically dedicated to the **Slovenian parish community**.
