# 🚀 New app: PrintPriceTool

We're excited to introduce **PrintPriceTool**, a new Treved app made for 3D printing enthusiasts, makers, and small businesses.

## 🖨️ What is PrintPriceTool?

PrintPriceTool helps you **calculate the real cost of your 3D prints** instead of relying on guesses.

It takes more than just filament into account. You can factor in:

- 🧵 Material and filament costs
- ⏱️ Printing time and your hourly rate
- ⚡ Electricity, maintenance, and other costs through a custom markup
- 💰 Your desired profit margin

This makes it easier to set prices that are transparent, consistent, and sustainable.

## 📊 Keep track of your projects

Your projects are stored locally, allowing you to keep a history of your calculations, edit previous projects, and manage your records.

You can also export your data to **CSV** for further bookkeeping and financial analysis.

For individual projects, PrintPriceTool can generate **professional PDF reports** containing details such as the project name, date, material usage, printing time, and cost breakdown.

## 🔒 Privacy & Offline

PrintPriceTool is designed with privacy in mind.

The app works **entirely offline**. Your projects are stored locally on your device, with no cloud storage, tracking, or collection of personal data. No registration is required, and the app doesn't require sensitive permissions such as access to your camera or contacts.

## 🌍 Available in multiple languages

PrintPriceTool currently supports **English, Slovenian, German, Spanish, and French**, with an option to follow your device's system language.
